Do I remain yet alive?

The imps of invisible illness ensnare me --
I cough and sputter through thick miasma --
The imps drag me again to the Gates of Hades,
And I am rebuffed when they again refuse to open.

My spine cracks like stacked stones --
My muscles ache and burn like cinders --
My hands tremble, and my fingers falter --
My legs lurch, and my feet fumble their foothold --
My heart hesitates, and my lungs rasp --
My eyes spin and turn grey --
From my mind, my image diminishes --
I stumble back from my instruments.

The sneering servants of sickness
Relax their grip upon me once more.
I open my eyes, and the greyness abates,
And I see again the walls of my corporeal prison.

My health has been expended
In pursuit of others' ends,
Yet I cannot gain recompense,
And when I do, naught is mended --
How can they tend to a flame
Lit by a smouldering spark?

Lo, these long years I have toiled in solitude --
My mind and my music were my only comforts,
And they speed away swiftly with the imps --
They are replaced by a dull and sessile tormentor:
A tearful repetition that numbs and sedates,
The summation of my incompletion -- a Great Nothing.

I am bound up in a broken basin --
Chaos leaks away through the cracks --
How shall I give birth to a dancing star?

I cry out to the gods; they withdraw quietly.
I cry out to the muse; she withers silently.
I cry out to ancestors; they regard me as a stranger.
My friends blink in deafness,
And my family speaks another language.

Do I remain yet alive?

Death has denied my supplications:
I entreated Death daily for years,
But my prayers for absolution were rejected --
I was condemned without explanation.

Am I more than a thinking urn?
Does a self yet remain to be overcome?
Or am I merely reanimated remains?
