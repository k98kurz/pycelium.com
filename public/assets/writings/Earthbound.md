I do not recall how I got here, and I do not remember much from my first few years on this planet. When I arrived, I was very small and fragile, but my slow adaptation was adequate to survive the subsequent decades in this planet's environment. However, this process was limited to biological adaptation to the material environment; I never quite managed to adapt to the social environments of the local dominant life forms.

Ever since I had reconstituted enough mental acuity to recognize my predicament, I have searched for a way off of this rock, a route to return home. Alas, I appear to be trapped in this world: my every attempt to leave has been thwarted by agents working in the shadows. I am trapped, and my biological vessel is now degrading. I fear that I may not get out of this place -- that when the biological vessel is finally allowed to fail, I will remain bound to this rock.

There is very little that makes any sense in this world. Everything is upside down: the incompetent rise to the top; the competent sink into a mire; the core systems of social organization are predicated upon lies, and though everyone can see they are lies, the majority continue to fall in line. I suspect the agents working in the shadows to thwart my attempts at escape may be agents of Archons who find my suffering here savory, but perhaps I have some other purpose here -- if I was to take the position that I have some mission to complete, it is to create an alternative set of systems that resolve the dissonance in the dominant social institutions of this world, a set of systems predicated upon virtue and truth instead of avarice and lies. However, I have received no explicit notification of any mission parameters; the only intelligible communications I have received have been spam messages from cosmic pests.

Six years ago, the planet endured an artificial catastrophe: some reckless researchers engineered a virus that caused a notable decline in the mental acuity of the people it infected, and the rulers responded by destroying the economy, promulgating bogus health advice, and creating a vaccine that did not prevent infection and carried with it the same spike proteins that caused blood clots and neuronal damage. The crisis lasted two to three years, with crisis responses having regional differences.

After the first year and a half of the crisis, I had a moment of inspiration while walking between the trees and the domeciles in a subtropical part of the planet: I saw in my mind's eye a new technological framework of self-organizing networks with cryptographic systems encoding the will and commitments of each individual person. This vision drove me through cycles of research, discovery, experimentation, and creation. I made substantial progress toward this vision. However, the last three years have been mired with substantial health issues apparently deriving from exposure to mycotoxins that have not been fully studied or understood; my every effort at recovery has been fruitless, and I have had to endure more months of aimless suffering in that time period than months of being able to work towards any goals. None of the medical professionals available have been of any avail.
